# proper-nouns.txt — everyday capitalised words that should count as answers.
#
# WHY this file exists: the big word lists (ENABLE, SCOWL) leave out every
# word that is written with a capital letter, so "April", "Monday", "Canada"
# and "French" were rejected even though everyone knows them. This list adds
# those back, but only in a few clear groups, picked by hand:
#   1. months, days and holidays
#   2. continents, regions, countries, nationalities and languages
#   3. US states, Canadian provinces, Australian states and big world cities
# First names and brands are left out on purpose (thousands of them, and no
# fair way to decide which count).
#
# Rules: one word per line, lowercase, letters only. Names made of two words
# ("New York", "Sri Lanka") can't be typed as one answer, so they're skipped.
# Lines starting with # are notes; the game skips them. Duplicates of words
# already in the other lists ("china", "turkey", "march") are harmless.
